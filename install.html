Install the VCE Attendance bookmark

A one-time setup. The bookmark reads your attendance on the ERP page, inside your own browser, and opens your tracker. Your ERP password is never used or collected.

On a laptop

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: VCE Attendance
  2. Log in to the VCE ERP and stay on the dashboard (the page listing your semesters).
  3. Click the bookmark once. Your tracker opens with your numbers.

On an Android phone (Chrome)

  1. Tap
  2. Open any page, tap the three dots, then the star to bookmark it. Open Bookmarks, edit that bookmark, set the name to vce, and paste the code into the URL box. Save.
  3. Log in to the VCE ERP and stay on the dashboard.
  4. Tap the address bar, type vce, and tap the suggestion with the bookmark icon.
Not working? Try the longer code

Some ERP pages block the short version. This one carries everything inside it.

Prefer not to use a bookmark? Open the tracker and paste your attendance page into it instead.
